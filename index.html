About Rwamagana Leaders' School

Discover our rich history, dedicated mission, and the exceptional team that makes Rwaamagana Leaders' School a leader in educational excellence.

Our Story

From humble beginnings to becoming a beacon of education in Rwanda, discover the inspiring journey of Rwamagana Leaders' School over the past two decades.

Beginning (2005)

Idea of the school begins after discussions with Rwandan educators following the 1994 Genocide.

Foundation (2006–2008)

Fundraising, non-profit registration, land purchase, and first volunteers.

First Classes (2009–2010)

English Enrichment Program starts with 23 students. Official opening and first classroom building completed.

Growth (2011–2014)

Expansion of facilities (kitchen, dorms, labs, cafeteria) and strong academic performance.

Expansion (2015–2018)

First graduating class. Girls' dorm, computer lab, solar power, and student achievements.

Transformation (2019–2020)

Name changed to Rwamagana Leaders' School; new leadership. COVID-19 response, community support, and boarding system.

Modern Development (2021–2023)

Campus expansion, new academic system, library, and admin building.

Impact & Inclusion (2023–2024)

Inclusion (disabled students), robotics recognition, university fair, endowment fund.

Vision (2025)

Vision to become a top school and grow from 200 to 400 students.

Join us in continuing this legacy of excellence and leadership.

Our Commitment

Rwamagana Leaders' School (RLS) is committed to building socially, environmentally, and economically sustainable education in Rwanda.

Our Pillars

School Culture

We foster a strong school culture rooted in respect, teamwork, mentorship, and character development. Through small mentorship groups called Crews, students grow academically and personally while learning to compete with their own standards rather than against each other.

Community Service

Community service is central to our mission. Students actively engage in meaningful projects that build long-term relationships and strengthen the surrounding community.

Sustainability

As a model of sustainability, our campus integrates rainwater harvesting, solar power, biogas systems, recycling, and small-scale agriculture. We are developing an A-Level curriculum that prepares students for future careers in green architecture, alternative energy, environmental law, water management, and other sustainability-focused fields.

Economic Independence

RLS aims for increasing economic independence through tuition contributions and income-generating projects while continuing to support students in need.

Expeditionary Learning

We use Expeditionary Learning (EL) to promote critical thinking, inquiry-based learning, strong literacy skills, and character development alongside academic excellence.

Legacy & Leadership

Founded in 2010 through the collaboration of Robin Strickler and Karl Smith, RLS was established to provide high-quality secondary education, with a special emphasis on girls and vulnerable students.
