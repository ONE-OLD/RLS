1. Privacy Policy

At Rwamagana Leaders' School (RLS), accessible from rls-roan.vercel.app, we prioritize the privacy of our visitors, students, and community members. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected and recorded by our platform and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Details: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or inquiry details submitted voluntarily via contact or admission forms.
  • Log Files: Standard browser details, IP addresses, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring pages, and exit pages to analyze trends and manage the site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our educational web portal.
  • Respond to parental, student, or community inquiries.
  • Improve user experience and website functionality.
  • Communicate important updates regarding academic admissions or school events.

Data Protection

We implement strict administrative and technical measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification of personal data. We do not sell or trade personal information to third parties.

3. Accessibility Statement

Rwamagana Leaders' School is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continuously improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance Status

We strive to adhere to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. Measures taken include:

  • Ensuring appropriate color contrast ratios for text readability across both light and dark themes.
  • Providing structured HTML markup for screen reader compatibility.
  • Including text alternatives (alt text) for visual media where applicable.
  • Ensuring full keyboard navigation capability across main navigation links.
